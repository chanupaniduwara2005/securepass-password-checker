📋 Password Security Best Practices

Actionable, defensive security guidelines aligned with modern NIST SP 800-63B standards. Say goodbye to outdated advice.

💬

1. Creating High-Entropy Passphrases

  • Choose 4 to 5 completely unrelated dictionary words (e.g., cobalt-turnip-galaxy-anchor).
  • Connect words with hyphens, underscores, or spaces to improve human legibility.
  • Avoid popular quotes, movie titles, song lyrics, and proverbs—attackers index cultural texts into dictionary lists.
  • Aim for at least 16 to 24 total characters for core accounts (primary email, bank, password manager).
🗄️

2. Use a Trusted Password Manager

  • Generate unique, random 20+ character passwords for every website you visit.
  • Eliminate password reuse—if one site is breached, your other accounts remain completely untouched.
  • Protect your vault with one memorable, highly secure master passphrase and MFA.
  • Browser auto-fill features protect against spoofed phishing URLs that mimic real domains.
🔑

3. Multi-Factor Authentication & Passkeys

  • Prefer Authenticator Apps (TOTP) or Hardware Keys (FIDO2) over SMS text messages to prevent SIM-swapping.
  • Adopt FIDO2 WebAuthn Passkeys wherever supported—they are cryptographically bound to the domain and cannot be phished.
  • Store one-time account recovery codes in a secure, encrypted offline file or a physical safe.
  • Never approve unexpected push notifications on your phone (MFA fatigue defense).
🛡️

4. Defeating Credential Stuffing

  • Never use the same password for personal banking, corporate email, and entertainment apps.
  • Criminals maintain automated bots that ingest leaked databases and spray credentials into hundreds of online portals.
  • Use email alias forwarding services to obscure your primary email address on secondary services.
  • Monitor breach alert services (e.g. HaveIBeenPwned) to know when a platform you use was compromised.
❓

5. Account Recovery & Security Questions

  • Do not answer security questions honestly with public facts (mother's maiden name, childhood street, high school).
  • Treat security questions like secondary random passwords: generate a random string and store it in your password manager.
  • Keep secondary recovery emails and phone numbers current so you never get locked out.
  • Change default administrative passwords on Wi-Fi routers, IoT cameras, and smart home appliances on day one.
🚨

6. Compromise Response Playbook

  • Avoid arbitrary 30-day password rotations—modern standards prove forced rotations cause humans to pick predictable patterns.
  • Rotate credentials immediately when an active data breach or unauthorized session alert occurs.
  • Use the "Log out of all other active sessions" button in the account settings after updating your password.
  • Review recent login activity and audit third-party app permissions connected to your account.
